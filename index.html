How to play
  • Each round shows one random letter, the same for everyone. Write an answer starting with it for every category.
  • Every box must be filled before you can submit.
  • The first player to submit ends the round for everyone, and all answers lock at once. Nobody can submit late.
  • If nobody submits, the round ends by itself after 15 minutes.
  • A correct answer that nobody else gave scores 10 points.
  • A correct answer that other players also gave scores 5 points for each player who gave it.
  • Wrong answers, answers starting with the wrong letter and empty boxes score 0, including any box you had not filled when the round ended.
  • Answers are checked against our word lists. If an answer is not in the list and the host has voting on (3 or more players), the other players vote. It counts if it gets at least 2 yes votes and more yes than no, and it then scores like any correct answer. You cannot vote on your own answers.
  • After the last round the highest total wins. If players tie, they share the win. Then everyone can vote on a rematch. It starts when more than half of the players vote yes and the host is still in the room.
