How to play
  • One letter per round. Fill every box with a word that starts with it.
  • The first to submit ends the round for everyone, or it ends after 15 minutes.
  • A unique correct answer scores 10, a shared one 5, wrong or empty 0.
  • Unknown words can be checked by another player, in a circle.
  • Highest total wins and ties share the win. Then everyone votes on a rematch: more than half must say yes.
